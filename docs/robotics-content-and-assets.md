# Robotics content and assets

Updated 5 November 2026.

## Company service scope

The user confirmed that robotics has two services: replacing old robot parts with new components (retrofitting), and manufacturing new robots from scratch. These are now a fourth service area across the site, a dedicated /robotics/ page and a homepage section.

The page explains general project considerations, assessment, component compatibility, new mechanical construction, controls and programming. No robot payload, accuracy, guaranteed cycle time, OEM authorisation, certification, warranty or completed client robotics project is claimed.

## Primary research

- [KUKA: Upgrade & Refurbish Services](https://www.kuka.com/en-at/services/service_robots-and-machines/refurbished-robots%2C-system-modernization%2C-retrofit-machines) — assessment, wear-part replacement, controller upgrades, mechanical/electrical changes and programming.
- [KUKA: Application and robot programming](https://www.kuka.com/en-de/services/service_roboter-und-maschinen/installation-start-up-and-programming-of-robots/application-and-robot-programming) — programming, adaptation, commissioning and production-system interfaces.
- [igus: Build with robotic components](https://www.igus.com/automation/end-to-end-automation) — custom robotic systems assembled from joints, gearboxes, drives, motors, cables and mechanical components.
- [igus: ReBeL integrated robotic gearbox](https://www.igus.com/product/rebel-gearbox) — the relationship between joint mechanics, motor drive, control electronics and custom robot configurations.

These sources inform general explanations. They are not evidence of a business affiliation between Linear Automation and the referenced manufacturers. Company-specific service claims come from the user's instructions.
